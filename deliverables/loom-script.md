# Loom script (about 75–85 seconds)

**Before you record:** open the demo on the Overview page, set 90d, All stores. Have the Creative & Team tab ready. Browser at 100% zoom, laptop size.

---

**[0:00, Overview, cursor on the KPI cards]**
"Hi Thimo, rather than just send a proposal, I built a small working version of what you described, so you can click around before we talk. It's all mock data for a made-up group with three stores, so nothing real is in here."

**[0:10, point at Revenue, then Blended ROAS next to Meta-reported ROAS]**
"Top line is total revenue across all stores, with spend, profit, orders and AOV, each compared with the previous period. Revenue comes straight from Shopify orders. And I show blended ROAS next to Meta-reported ROAS on purpose. Here Meta claims about 19% more than the bank account sees, and that's the kind of gap a dashboard should make obvious."

**[0:25, click 30d, then click "Glow Lab" in the store table]**
"Every view filters by store and date. Click a store and the whole dashboard follows."

**[0:32, go to Creative & Team, cursor on the blue Winner rule bar]**
"This is the part I think matters most for your team. Win rate only means something once you agree what a winner is. Right now it's ROAS of 2 or more after €300 spend within 7 days…"

**[0:42, click + on ROAS three times to 2.3, watch the leaderboard reorder]**
"…but watch what happens if I make it stricter. Every win rate recalculates live, and the leaderboard re-ranks: Anna moves up two places, Marco drops. So the rule itself is something we'd lock in with you on day one."

**[0:55, hover the (i) next to Creatives, then click a row to open the history]**
"Strategist and angle aren't tagged by hand. They're read from the ad name, like AH, ugc-pain, serum, v3. Click any creative and you see its day-by-day history against the rule."

**[1:05, go to Angles & Insights, point at the three cards, then the matrix]**
"Then angles. Win rate and ROAS per angle, how each angle does in each store, and these three insight cards are calculated from the data, not written by me. For example, founder-story ads win about three times more often on one store than on the others."

**[1:18, open Data plan briefly]**
"Last tab is how I'd build the real thing: Shopify as the source of truth, no duplicate orders, full history, rate limits handled, all in a clear four-phase plan. I'd start with an audit of your reference dashboard. Happy to walk through it whenever suits you."

---

**Notes**
- If you change the default filters while rehearsing, numbers in the script (19%, "moves up two places", "three times") may differ. They match 90d · All stores for the overview and insights, and 90d · All stores · ROAS 2.3 for the leaderboard.
- Filters and the rule are remembered in your browser. Click **Reset** on the rule bar before recording.
